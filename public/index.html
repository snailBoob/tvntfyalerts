Connect to TradingView

Paste the two session cookies from your browser. You do not need your TradingView password.

This server stores your cookies and sends them only to TradingView. Your cookies never appear in ntfy or the logs.

Where do I find the cookies?

  1. Open TradingView in Brave or Chrome.
  2. Sign in to TradingView.
  3. Open Developer Tools with F12 or through the browser menu.
  4. Select Application → Storage → Cookies.
  5. Select TradingView.
  6. Copy the Value of sessionid into the sessionid field.
  7. Copy the Value of sessionid_sign into the sessionid_sign field.

If your session is no longer valid:

  1. Click Open settings in the ntfy notification.
  2. Paste fresh cookies.
  3. Click Connect again.
