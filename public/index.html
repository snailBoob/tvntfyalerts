Connect to TradingView

Paste the two session cookies from your browser. Your TradingView password is not needed.

Cookies are stored on this server and sent only to TradingView. They never appear in ntfy or the logs.

Where do I find the cookies?

  1. Open TradingView in Brave or Chrome and sign in.
  2. Open Developer Tools with F12 or through the browser menu.
  3. Select Application → Storage → Cookies, then TradingView.
  4. Copy the Value of sessionid and sessionid_sign into the matching fields.

Session no longer valid? Use Open settings in the ntfy notification, paste fresh cookies and click Connect again.
